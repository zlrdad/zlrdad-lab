01 — The sector at a glance

Canada's most strategic growth sector — and Ontario is its engine.

Canada launched the world's first national AI strategy in 2017, and the field's founding figures — Hinton, Bengio, Sutton — built their work here. Information-and-communications technology has climbed from 3.2% to 5.4% of GDP since 2000, and Ontario accounts for roughly half of Canada's entire AI economic contribution.

02 — How big it is

A knowledge economy worth six figures of billions.

Ontario's official accounts track the sector through knowledge-service proxies. Together they exceed 16% of provincial GDP — and the centre of gravity has shifted decisively from hardware to software and services.
