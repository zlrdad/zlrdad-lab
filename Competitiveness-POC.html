About this briefing

Generated from the International Comparator Data Compendium in an Obsidian research vault. Every figure is drawn from a single validated data layer; click any ◎ source chip to see the originating section and citation, and the confidence the underlying data carries.

Confidence is graded honestly: hard government/IMF/WIPO figures are marked high; single-year VC totals and per-capita unicorn counts are marked soft because the source data itself varies by methodology. CAD conversions are approximate. This is a proof-of-concept built to demonstrate the format — not a published report.
